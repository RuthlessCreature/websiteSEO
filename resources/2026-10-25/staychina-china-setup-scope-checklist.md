# China company-setup scope checklist for an initial consultation

**Status: editorial draft; not published.** This checklist helps a business explain what it is trying to establish and identify questions for appropriately qualified advisers. It is not a legal checklist of mandatory documents, a registration instruction, immigration advice or a promise that any application will be approved.

## A. Describe the business decision

- Proposed China activity, products/services and revenue model
- Intended customers and counterparties
- Target city or cities and why they are being considered
- Expected local operating location, if known
- Proposed ownership and control structure at a high level
- Target decision date and expected first operating date
- Questions about activities, sectors or locations that still need official confirmation

Do not send passport numbers, bank records, personal addresses or other sensitive documents in an initial marketing inquiry. Share personal or corporate records only through a verified service channel after the recipient and purpose are understood.

## B. Identify the people and business roles

| Role to clarify | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Foreign parent / investor | Which entity is considering the activity? Who can authorize consultation? |
| China operating entity | Is an entity already established? If so, what is its registered name and current status? |
| Local responsible person | Who can coordinate locally and confirm the location/activity? |
| Employer | Which legal entity would employ any China-based staff? |
| Proposed employee | What role, work location, employment relationship and planned start window are under consideration? Do not send identity documents at this stage. |
| Professional adviser | Which question requires a lawyer, accountant, licensed service provider or government-office confirmation? |

Company setup, employing staff, work authorization and residence matters may involve different parties and processes. Confirm current requirements and responsibilities for the specific case with the relevant official authority and qualified adviser before relying on any general guide.

## C. Sort questions into the right verification lane

For each open question, record the responsible source, location, date checked, exact page/document, adviser and next action.

| Question | Verification owner | Source and date | What remains undecided |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is the intended business activity permitted for this structure and location? | Relevant market-regulation / sector authority with qualified counsel | To verify for the proposed activity and city | Do not infer from another company's experience |
| What filings or registrations apply? | Competent registration authority and qualified adviser | To verify for the specific scope | Do not treat a generic document list as exhaustive |
| Which tax, accounting or capital questions apply? | Qualified tax/accounting adviser and relevant authority | To verify by entity and transaction | Keep commercial estimates separate from official obligations |
| Who would be the actual employer and work location? | Proposed employer and relevant human-resources adviser | To confirm before an employment filing | Do not promise a work permit or visa outcome |
| Which work/residence process applies to a person? | Relevant immigration/public-security authority and qualified adviser | Verify current policy and local handling | Individual factors may change the answer |

## D. Prepare a useful first meeting

Bring a one-page business description, city options, an organization-chart sketch, expected activity, desired decision timeline, known official documents without sensitive identifiers, and a list of unresolved questions. For staff planning, first confirm the employing entity and role before assuming any specific work-permit route.

Ask the service provider to state clearly:

1. Which organization or official source owns the final decision?
2. Which steps it performs and which the client, employer or government office performs?
3. What current rule/source/date supports each procedural statement?
4. What facts or documents are needed for a case-specific review, and how will they be handled?
5. What fees, exclusions, assumptions and dependencies apply before any engagement?

## Reader next step

Separate the project into business registration, operations, employment and individual immigration questions. Take each question to the body or professional qualified to answer it for the actual activity and location.
