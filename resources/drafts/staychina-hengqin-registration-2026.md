# Hengqin company registration in 2026: official routes and document checks

**Editorial status:** Draft for source and legal review. Not published. Checked 26 October 2026.

**Audience:** Foreign founders and businesses assessing whether to establish an entity in Hengqin Guangdong-Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone.

**Scope:** This guide describes where to verify the current registration route and what changed in Hengqin's published process. It is not legal, tax, immigration or investment advice. Hengqin-specific procedures do not determine whether a business is permitted nationally or whether an investor qualifies for a special policy.

## Start with two separate questions

Before preparing incorporation papers, answer these separately:

1. Is the proposed business activity permitted under the national market-access rules and, where foreign investment is involved, the foreign-investment access rules?
2. Which current Hengqin registration route, form set and document list applies to the proposed entity and its investors?

A local registration form does not resolve the first question. The National Development and Reform Commission's 2025 National Negative List for Market Access applies to domestic and foreign-invested entities. Foreign-invested enterprises must also check the Special Administrative Measures (Negative List) for Foreign Investment, whose nationwide 2024 edition took effect on 1 November 2024. Check for later amendments before filing.

## Use the current Hengqin forms

Hengqin's notice dated 30 April 2026 says the zone began using the 2026 enterprise-registration application forms and document-materials list on 1 May 2026. Download the current forms and list from the official notice or the linked government service system rather than reusing an older checklist copied from another city or year.

The government's registration guide directs applicants to the Guangdong government service portal, select Hengqin, then follow the applicable domestic-invested or foreign-invested enterprise establishment route. Confirm the route shown by the live portal for the actual ownership structure; do not infer the route solely from the founder's nationality or the intended brand name.

## A practical preparation sequence

1. **Describe the activity precisely.** Write down the actual products/services, operating model, customers and any regulated elements. Compare the activity with the current national market-access list and the foreign-investment negative list where applicable.
2. **Confirm the entity and investor route.** Use the current Hengqin/Guangdong government portal and the 2026 forms. Ask the registration authority which route applies if the ownership or document situation is unclear.
3. **Build the filing set from the live materials list.** Check the exact form version, signatures, identity and authorization documents, translations or notarizations if required, and any investor-specific attachments against the current official list. Requirements can vary by structure and case.
4. **Submit through the stated official channel.** Save the submission receipt and any correction notice. Use the authority's current instructions for online filing or an in-person service window.
5. **Verify any agent.** Hengqin's August 2026 notice says registration agents must be entered in the national agent-management system. Ask for the agent's registration details and verify them independently. The same notice cautions against claims such as “all materials handled,” “expedited,” or “guaranteed approval.”
6. **Plan post-registration obligations separately.** Registration is only one step. Tax, banking, licensing, employment, work/residence permissions and ongoing reporting have their own authorities and requirements.

## Check source and date before relying on a rule

For each requirement, record the issuing authority, document title, jurisdiction, effective date and the date you checked it. If an official page links to a newer form or notice, use that newer source. A guide or service provider cannot guarantee approval, processing time, work permission, tax treatment or access to a local incentive.
