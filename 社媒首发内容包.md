## Xiaodu：首发内容

### 1. 机器人自动化项目先做什么（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

Before selecting a robot, map the whole workcell: the part and its variation, takt time, upstream/downstream equipment, operator access, safety interlocks, changeover needs and acceptance criteria. These details determine whether the project needs a robot alone or a coordinated cell with fixtures, vision and controls.

Start with the process and interfaces, then size the automation scope. Xiaodu’s overview: <https://xiaodu.tech/en/solutions/robotic-automation/>

#IndustrialAutomation #Robotics #Manufacturing

**中文**

做机器人自动化，先梳理整个工位：工件及变化范围、节拍、前后工序设备、人员操作位置、安全联锁、换型方式和验收条件。这些信息决定项目需要单台机器人，还是要把夹具、视觉、控制与外围设备一起规划。

从工艺和接口入手，再确定自动化边界。解决方案介绍：<https://xiaodu.tech/zh-cn/solutions/robotic-automation/>

### 2. 机器视觉检测要先定义判定规则（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

A camera does not define a quality standard. Before building an inspection station, specify the defect classes, acceptable limits, lighting and part presentation, pass/fail logic, recheck path and what evidence must be stored. Then connect the inspection result to the line and traceability system.

A useful vision project starts with the decision the process needs to make. <https://xiaodu.tech/en/solutions/machine-vision/>

#MachineVision #QualityEngineering #SmartManufacturing

**中文**

相机本身不等于质量标准。规划视觉检测前，先说明缺陷类别、可接受边界、光照与工件姿态、判定逻辑、复判流程和需要保存的证据，再决定如何把结果接入产线与追溯系统。

机器视觉项目应先从现场要做出的判断开始定义。<https://xiaodu.tech/zh-cn/solutions/machine-vision/>

### 3. 自动化与设备数据如何衔接（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

Automation and industrial data should be planned together. Agree on equipment states, alarm meaning, timestamps, batch or part identifiers, system interfaces and who will use each view. This makes production monitoring and maintenance records easier to interpret after commissioning.

Xiaodu’s industrial software and data integration scope: <https://xiaodu.tech/en/solutions/industrial-software-data/>

#IndustrialData #Automation #Maintenance

**中文**

自动化和工业数据最好一起规划。先统一设备状态、报警含义、时间戳、批次或工件标识、系统接口，以及每类数据由谁使用。这样调试交接后，生产监控和维护记录才更容易被正确理解。

工业软件与数据平台介绍：<https://xiaodu.tech/zh-cn/solutions/industrial-software-data/>

## Pomerol：首发内容

### 1. RFQ 的目标是让报价可以比较（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

A useful RFQ gives suppliers the same baseline: specification revision, quantity by SKU, packaging, tooling assumptions, delivery terms, lead time, quality evidence and required documents. Ask suppliers to separate one-time costs and list exclusions. Then a price comparison becomes a comparison of the same job.

Pomerol’s practical RFQ guide: <https://pomerol.trade/resources/guides/china-rfq-template-guide/>

#ChinaSourcing #Procurement #RFQ

**中文**

一份可用的 RFQ，要让供应商围绕同一口径报价：规格版本、各 SKU 数量、包装、模具假设、贸易条款、交期、质量证据和文件要求。要求单列一次性费用并说明排除项，报价才有实际可比性。

中国供应商 RFQ 指南：<https://pomerol.trade/zh/resources/guides/china-rfq-template-guide/>

### 2. 样品批准要留下量产基准（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

A sample approval should record what was actually accepted: material, dimensions, finish, components, function, packaging and revision. If any item changes before production, capture the change and re-approve the relevant baseline. That record gives both the buyer and supplier a common reference for inspection.

Pomerol’s sample-approval guide: <https://pomerol.trade/resources/guides/china-sample-approval-process/>

#OEM #QualityControl #ChinaProcurement

**中文**

样品批准应记录具体批准了什么：材料、尺寸、表面处理、部件、功能、包装和版本。量产前如有变化，要留下变更记录并重新确认相应基准。验货时，买卖双方才能对照同一份已批准资料。

样品批准流程：<https://pomerol.trade/zh/resources/guides/china-sample-approval-process/>

### 3. 多供应商集货需要统一数据（LinkedIn / 知乎 / 微信）

**English**

Consolidating goods from several factories is a coordination job. Agree on delivery windows, SKU and carton labels, warehouse receiving records, final quantities, packing-list ownership and freight handoff. Separate basic receiving from any inspection, relabeling or repacking so responsibilities and exceptions stay visible.

Pomerol’s consolidation guide: <https://pomerol.trade/resources/guides/consolidate-shipments-from-china/>

#SupplyChain #ExportLogistics #ChinaSourcing

**中文**

多家工厂集货，核心是把数据和交接统一起来：到货窗口、SKU 与箱唛、仓库收货记录、最终数量、装箱单责任人和货代交接。基础收货、验货、换标与重新包装应分别约定，异常才容易追踪。

多供应商集货指南：<https://pomerol.trade/zh/resources/guides/consolidate-shipments-from-china/>
